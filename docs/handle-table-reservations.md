**Reservations** shows the table requests customers make. Each one waits for you to confirm or decline it.

## Your views

- **Schedule** is the diary. Slots are coloured **Waiting for you**, **Confirmed**, **Arrived**, **Not taking bookings**, **Closed** and **Turnaround** (a table being reset).
- **Live** shows what is happening now.

## Answer a request

In the **Reservations** list each request shows its status, when, the customer, how many people and the table.

- **Confirm** accepts it. If you entered an email, the customer is sent a confirmation.
- **Decline** turns it down.
- **Arrived** marks that the party has turned up.
- **Edit** changes the details, and **Cancel reservation** cancels a confirmed one.
- **Delete** moves it to **Deleted Reservations**, where **Purge All** removes them for good.

## Add one yourself

Press **New reservation** and enter the **Customer name**, **Phone**, **Email (to send confirmation)**, number of **People** and the **Table**.
